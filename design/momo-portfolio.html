About

I'm Cheng-Mou (Momo) Chen 陳政謀, a sophomore at the McCombs School of Business at UT Austin, planning to declare Finance alongside a BA in Economics. I build things where capital markets, technology, and the rules that govern them overlap.

My work runs in three directions: quantitative projects like a factor-investing backtest engine, AI tools built for a research team at the UT School of Nursing, and legal writing on securities regulation and AI copyright. Quantitative finance

Factor Investing Backtest Engine

More than 10 years of S&P 500 data, tested factor by factor. I built quintile long-short portfolios on momentum, value, size, and quality, plus a multi-factor composite.

  • Reports Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown, and factor correlations for each strategy.
  • An 8-stage ETL pipeline makes sure every signal uses only data available at the time, so results carry no look-ahead bias.

Python, SQL/SQLite, pandas, yfinance, matplotlib
